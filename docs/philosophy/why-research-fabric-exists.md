# Why ResearchFabric Exists

Research often fragments across notebooks, chats, repositories, citation managers, simulation output, and individual memory. AI assistance adds another fast-moving surface. Useful work can become trapped in a session, and a confident synthesis can be mistaken for evidence.

ResearchFabric makes continuity a first-class design concern. It gives durable homes to reusable knowledge and executable project inquiry while keeping the boundary between them visible. The “fabric” is not a centralized service: it is the set of traceable relationships among questions, sources, methods, evidence, terminology, and decisions.

The aim is not maximum automation. It is better scientific memory, clearer handoffs, and safer acceleration.
