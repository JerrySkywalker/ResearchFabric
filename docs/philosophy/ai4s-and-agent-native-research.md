# AI4S and Agent-Native Research

ResearchFabric is an opinionated approach to AI4S infrastructure. “Agent-native” means research state is shaped so that capable agents can inspect and act on it without becoming its authority. Explicit files, metadata, boundaries, and validation make admitted work reproducible by another agent or by a human.

Agents are replaceable substrates. They may retrieve literature, draft code, run bounded experiments, compare evidence, search for counterexamples, and propose interpretations. Their identity is not the research identity. Their output becomes durable only through the same provenance, review, and authority rules applied to other contributions.

This is augmentation, not replacement. Human scientific judgment decides what questions matter, what risks are acceptable, what evidence is persuasive, and what conclusions are adopted.
